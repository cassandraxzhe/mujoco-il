# Thesis figures

Generated by `scripts/make_figures.py`. Each entry is saved as both
`.pdf` (for thesis) and `.png` (for browsing). Regenerate with:

```bash
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/make_figures.py           # all figures
PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/make_figures.py a c j     # subset
```

Data dependencies:
- Trained weights under `experiments/weights/` and `experiments/ppo/<run>/`
- Hardware-data-derived normalisation files alongside each .pt
- Stair XMLs auto-regenerated into `/tmp/…xml`

## Index

### Primary comparison figures

**bar_comparison.pdf** — Two-panel bar chart. Panel (a) is max forward
distance on a symlog axis with ✓ annotations over bars that crossed the
3-step flight; panel (b) is episode length as a stability proxy.
Source: §32 comparison table in `progress.md`. Values hardcoded from
`scripts/compare_controllers.py` output.

**com_trajectory.pdf** — Top: x–z side view of each controller's torso
trajectory on the 3-step flight, with step geometry drawn as grey
boxes. Bottom: x vs time. Expert, IL, and both stair-aware PPO
variants cross all three steps; PPO flat-only fails at step 3.

**flight_height_sweep.pdf** — Robustness across flight step heights
(6, 8, 10, 12 mm). Panel (a): grouped bars of max x per controller per
height. Panel (b): crossing-success heatmap (red = failed, green =
crossed). Shows expert + IL robust at all heights; PPO flat fails
everywhere; PPO stair & mix cross 6–10 mm but fail at 12 mm.

### Training figures

**learning_curves.pdf** — PPO reward and episode length over timesteps
for `ppo_v2`, `ppo_stair_v1`, `ppo_mix_v1`. From tensorboard logs.

**multi_seed_curves.pdf** — Mean ± std of reward and episode length
across 3 random seeds for `ppo_mix` at 1 M steps. For the thesis's
variability claims.

### Behavioural-characterisation figures

**hop_cycles.pdf** — z-vs-time for expert, IL, PPO mix on flat forward
motion. Shows expert & IL hop at z_des=8 cm apex; PPO mix hovers at a
lower amplitude. One-picture summary of the aggression/conservatism
distinction in §32.

**apex_histogram.pdf** — 10 s of hover: apex-height histograms for
expert (μ=8.16, σ=0.11 cm), IL (μ=7.96, σ=0.98 cm), PPO mix
(μ=5.69, σ=0.82 cm). Expert is the tightest; PPO undershoots
target systematically.

**per_wing_forces.pdf** — 4-panel time-series of f1–f4 during 1.5 s of
forward hopping. Expert and IL nearly identical bang-bang-with-pause
pattern; PPO mix produces noisier, higher-frequency commands. Visual
evidence that IL clones the expert at the per-wing level while PPO
has a qualitatively different control style.

### Methods figures

**stair_schematic.pdf** — Side-view reference for the 3-step flight
geometry (h = 8 mm, d = 20 mm, cumulative), with labelled dimensions
and the hopper start position indicator. Use in methods section.

**stair_height_sweep.pdf** — Single-step crossing vs step height (2 to
25 mm), plotted as max forward x reached. Tests the narrow "one step"
task rather than the full flight. Expert reaches 100 cm at h = 8 mm;
all controllers fail at h ≥ 20 mm (step taller than foot clearance).
